Kawasaki KLE500

Adventure Motorcycle · 2026
Road test & launch stills · BoxzaRacing
Red-dirt trail, lakeside roads, open tarmac

Kawasaki brought the KLE name back as a rally-styled 451cc adventure bike, and the pictures had to sell both halves of that — dirt and distance. Low wide-angle heroes from the ground so the bike owns the sky, slow-shutter pans and tracking shots on the back roads, roost and dust on the trail. White, green and a lot of blue.

Kawasaki Z650RS

Retro Roadster · 2027 model, new colour
Shot for BoxzaRacing · used by Kawasaki in its launch campaign
Japanese-town set, low sun

Navy and gold, a round headlamp and a 649cc parallel twin — a modern bike dressed as a 1970s Z. I shot it like a period piece: low angles against timber eaves and tiled roofs, the bike framed through doorways, and the tank badge filling the frame. Kawasaki picked the photographs up for its Z650RS “Retrovolution” launch posts on Facebook and on the web.

Range Rover Sport

Luxury Performance SUV · Land Rover · 2026
Road test & launch stills · BoxzaRacing
Gravel, lakeside & highway

A big, quiet, expensive car that still has to look fast. Details first — the grille lettering, the light bar — then statics on gravel by the water and rolling shots on the highway and under the flyover, with a few frames pulled from the motion pass. The paint does a lot of the work: cool sage-blue that changes with every sky.

Experience · Documentary Photography

Documentary

2 photo essays · Sarakadee Magazine
Issue 477 · December 2024

Two photo essays from the 19th Sarakadee Documentary Photography Camp, made in pairs — a photographer working alongside a writer — and published online and in the magazine's December 2024 print edition. Documentary work is decided by its first and last frame: the opener has to be beautiful enough to pull a reader in, the closer has to say what the whole story was about. Everything between is patience.

Thai Vajrayana Buddhismพุทธวัชรยานไทย

Photo Essay · Sarakadee Magazine
Issue 477 · December 2024 · pp. 104–117
All photographs · shot alongside a writer

"Beneath the Spirit of the Yogis" — days inside Thailand's small Vajrayana community, following the lunar calendar from the first waxing night: ten thousand lamps offered to Amitabha, an unfinished stupa standing over the fields at Khadiravana, lay practitioners in maroon robes, the smoke of a ritual at dusk. A demanding shoot — the light was whatever the rooms and the hours gave: candle flame, overcast, a single window. Street instincts — timing, geometry, waiting for the figure to land — carried over, but bent to serve the story rather than the frame. The opening and closing images were planned first; everything else was earned in between.

Live Well, Die Wellอยู่ดี ตายดี ที่อุทัยธานี

Photo Essay · Sarakadee Magazine
Issue 477 · December 2024 · pp. 122–123
One spread of a group feature by the camp team

A feature on Uthai Thani, a river town where people seem to live well and grow old well. My spread follows Sinthuchai Phunkaew — once a ranger and a well-known boxer, now one of the last people living on a wooden raft house on the Sakae Krang — setting his fish trap in the morning, rowing the boat he still uses every day, the old fight photograph that hangs on his wall. Shot in a single visit, with the writer beside me; the rest of the feature was photographed by the camp team.

About

Hello, I'm Ohm

A Communication Arts graduate (Film major) from Silpakorn University, now working as a Director of Photography and photographer at BoxzaRacing — covering race weekends, drivers and new-vehicle launches — while still taking on DP work for short films and commercials.

My work moves between motorsport, commercial and narrative — from race weekends on the street circuit at Songkhla and launch shoots for Kawasaki and Land Rover, to a feature documentary for beIN Sports, an iPhone-shot campaign for Apple Thailand and photo essays in Sarakadee Magazine.

I shoot with a documentary instinct and light with cinematic intent. Whether it's a handheld interview or a controlled set, the goal is the same: images that hold attention and mean something.
