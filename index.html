About

Hello, I'm Ohm

A Communication Arts graduate (Film major) from Silpakorn University, working as a freelance Director of Photography and photographer.

My work moves between documentary, commercial and narrative — from a feature documentary for beIN Sports to an iPhone-shot campaign for Apple Thailand, to street photography published in Sarakadee Magazine.

I shoot with a documentary instinct and light with cinematic intent. Whether it's a handheld interview or a controlled set, the goal is the same: images that hold attention and mean something.
